# Sources — news of Sunday, 22 November 2026

Every story in the image was first reported, confirmed, or updated on
Sunday, 22 November 2026. Figures are as reported that day; where counts
were still changing (missing people, body bags), the image uses the
figures the sources gave on Sunday.

1. **COP31 collapses — Antalya, Turkey**
   - CNN, "Global climate summit collapses as countries fail to agree on
     moving away from fossil fuels", 22 Nov 2026.
   - Reuters (via WTBX), "COP31 climate talks collapse as EU and others
     reject deal", 22 Nov 2026.
   - Le Monde, "COP31: International community splits over phasing out
     fossil fuels and climate adaptation funding", 22 Nov 2026.
   - Facts used: no agreement after two weeks of talks and two days of
     overtime; longest COP on record; first summit to end without a deal
     since Copenhagen 2009; EU, Panama, Marshall Islands and Norway among
     those rejecting the "Antalya Pact" for omitting fossil fuels.

2. **Russian drone hits apartment block — Dymer, Kyiv region, Ukraine**
   - Reuters, "At least 14 people missing after Russian drone hit
     building in Kyiv region, Zelenskiy says", 22 Nov 2026.
   - Facts used: five-storey block partly collapsed on Sunday morning;
     14 people unaccounted for; nine evacuated; a 7-year-old boy pulled
     alive from the rubble after about 14 hours.

3. **Iran strikes Bahrain — two injured**
   - Reuters (via LA Post), "Bahrain says Iran strikes industrial
     facility as air defences intercept attacks", 21–22 Nov 2026.
   - Facts used: Iranian missile-and-drone attack on US positions
     (Al-Riffa and Awali bases) and a civilian industrial facility on
     Sitra on Saturday 21 Nov; on Sunday 22 Nov Bahrain confirmed two
     people injured, one in critical condition; Gulf states condemned
     the attacks.

4. **Candy factory fire — Cikande, Banten, Indonesia**
   - Reuters (via LA Post), "Indonesia rescuers search for 36 missing
     after factory fire kills 3", 22 Nov 2026; Associated Press.
   - Facts used: fire broke out Saturday 21 Nov at the PT Fonusa Agung
     Mulia factory; on Sunday 22 Nov rescuers searched for 36 missing
     workers, 3 confirmed dead, body bags recovered, DNA identification
     under way.

5. **BepiColombo reaches Mercury**
   - Reuters, "Spacecraft begins orbit of Mercury, with a glitch
     reported", 22 Nov 2026; ESA.
   - Facts used: after an eight-year journey the European-Japanese craft
     entered Mercury orbit (confirmed Saturday 21 Nov, reported Sunday);
     the thruster burn was shorter than planned after a software issue;
     the spacecraft is safe.

6. **Three Russian missiles entered Poland**
   - Reuters, "Three Russian missiles flew into Poland Friday,
     prosecutors say", 22 Nov 2026.
   - Facts used: three Russian Kh-101 missiles flew into Polish airspace
     on Friday; confirmed by Polish prosecutors on Sunday; a NATO North
     Atlantic Council meeting was set for Monday in Brussels.
